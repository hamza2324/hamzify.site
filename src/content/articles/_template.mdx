Copy a type-specific template instead of starting from a blank page:

- `_template-review.mdx`
- `_template-comparison.mdx`
- `_template-build-log.mdx`
- `_template-experiment.mdx`
- `_template-workflow.mdx`

Files that start with `_` are ignored by the content layer.

Write the brief first (see `docs/EDITORIAL.md` and `docs/PUBLISHING.md`). The
introduction should establish what was tested or built, and what this piece
specifically covers. Put a direct takeaway near the top.

Do not use a markdown `#` heading. The page template owns the H1.

Do not add an FAQ unless the questions are real, unanswered in the body, and
useful to a reader.
